q: how do i play?
a: click the roll button! higher luck multipliers increase your odds of pulling rarer items.
q: how does auto-roll work?
a: auto-roll automatically triggers rolls on cooldown so you don't have to keep clicking manually.
q: what is luck multiplier?
a: luck boosts your base roll probability curve. shop upgrades, potions, anomalies, and starmap perks increase luck.
q: how do mutations work?
a: unlock mutations via gauntlets, then combine two non-excluded rarities to discover new outcomes.
