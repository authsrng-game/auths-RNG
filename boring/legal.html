who we are
auth's RNG is a free browser-based game operated by Ivy Lopez, who develops and publishes the game under the name auth. all references to "auth," "we," "us," or "the developer" throughout this policy refer to Ivy Lopez as the sole operator of the service. Ivy Lopez is an individual based in canada. this privacy policy explains how data is handled when you play at authsrng.xyz and any subdomains (nightly.authsrng.xyz, backup.authsrng.xyz, leaderboard.authsrng.xyz, accounts.authsrng.xyz).
tl;dr: you can play the whole game without an account, and then your game data lives only in your own browser. creating an account is optional and is what turns on cloud saves, the leaderboard, and social features like friends and messaging. no ads, no analytics, no data selling, nothing sketchy at all.

what data we collect
data stored only in your browser (we never see this unless you are logged in to an account, which syncs it as described below):
  • your rarity inventory, roll count, achievements, shop upgrades, potions, anomalies, gauntlet progress, wishing well history, daily/weekly streak info, and notification history, saved to your browser's localStorage

  • profile info you choose to add: bio, pronouns, avatar image, banner image, profile theme colors, your layout preferences, social handles (discord, youtube, tiktok, github, twitch) and one custom link. all optional and editable/removable at any time
  • session data: the device/browser type, a creation time and a rough "last active" time for each device you're logged into (up to 20 per account), so you can review and revoke sessions from your account settings

  • inbox messages the developer sends you (for example account notices)
  • cloud save data: a copy of your game progress and some game preferences, kept in step with your local save automatically, plus restore points and a label for the device that last saved (the browser type and a random id). see cloud saves & restore points
  • leaderboard stats, if you opt in: total rolls, rarities collected, rarest rarity, playtime, points, and unlocked achievements, tied to your account username
  • linked discord account, if you link one: your discord user id and discord username, and when you linked it
  • reports and moderation records: see reports & moderation
data collected automatically (server logs):
  • standard web server logs may temporarily capture ip addresses and user-agent strings as part of normal http operations. these are not processed for profiling, are not sold or shared, and are rotated regularly.
  • ip addresses are also used in memory to rate-limit requests. they are not stored with your account.
we do not use analytics platforms, advertising networks, fingerprinting scripts, or third-party tracking. external services loaded by the game are listed in the third-party services section below.
accounts
creating an account is optional. you can play the entire game without one. an account is needed for cloud saves, the leaderboard, and social features (friends, messaging, public profiles).
  • passwords are hashed before storage; we cannot see or recover your actual password
  • backup keys let you reset your password if you forget it. each key works once. save them somewhere safe when shown, since we cannot show them to you again after that point
  • you can review and revoke individual active sessions (logged-in devices) from your account settings at any time
  • you can delete your account at any time. doing so permanently removes your cloud save and every restore point, your leaderboard entry, avatar, banner, profile customization, social links and linked discord details from our servers. your local, in-browser save is not affected. message history with other players is not deleted, since it's shared data belonging to a conversation, but your account will show as "[deleted user]" in any remaining threads. a few records can outlive deletion, as described under data retention
how we use your data
we use data only to provide the game's features:
  • cloud save data and restore points are used to sync your progress between devices and to recover it when you or the developer need to undo a mistake
  • leaderboard data is displayed publicly on the leaderboard page and your profile, as you agreed when opting in
  • a linked discord id is used only to give you roll milestone roles in the auth's RNG discord server
  • reports, moderation records and recent account activity are used to review abuse and keep the community safe
  • server logs are used only for diagnosing technical issues and maintaining uptime
we do not use your data for advertising, profiling, marketing, analytics, or selling to third parties. ever.
where data is stored
there are two distinct hosting layers:
  • game files (static frontend): authsrng.xyz is hosted on cloudflare pages, a global edge network. the game's html, js, and assets are served from cloudflare's infrastructure worldwide, not from a single server in canada.
  • backend data (accounts, cloud saves & leaderboard): stored in databases on servers operated by the developer, physically located in canada, and accessed via a cloudflare tunnel (no port forwarding). the developer makes daily backups of these databases on the same infrastructure.
because the frontend is served via cloudflare pages and all backend traffic routes through a cloudflare tunnel, cloudflare's infrastructure processes every request made to the game, including ip addresses and request headers, subject to cloudflare's privacy policy. cloudflare acts as a data processor on our behalf and is not permitted to use your data for its own purposes.
cloud saves and messages are protected in transit by https, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. that means the developer can technically access them on the server, and we only do so for support, moderation, and fixing problems.
your local game data never leaves your device unless you log in to an account.

ABSOLUTELY NOT. we do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third parties, advertising companies, data brokers, or anyone else. the only so-called "sharing" that occurs is:
  • leaderboard stats are displayed publicly on the leaderboard page and on your public profile (only if you opted in)
  • things you choose to make public on your profile (avatar, banner, bio, pronouns, social links, rough online status) are visible to anyone with the link

cloud saves & restore points
cloud saves require an account. while you are logged in, your save syncs automatically:
  • what is synced: your game progress (inventory, rolls, achievements, shop, potions, runes, starmap, expeditions, and similar progress data) plus a few game preferences such as theme editor presets, the start animation setting and notification history. it is stored as a compressed copy tied to your account
  • what is not synced: your password or login tokens, your general settings, and custom music files. these stay on your device
  • when it syncs: shortly after your progress changes, when you leave the page, and when you open the game on another device. only one open tab at a time saves, so an older copy cannot overwrite a newer one
  • conflicts: if two devices changed your save, the game shows both and you choose which to keep. the one you don't keep is stored as a restore point
  • protections: the server rejects automatic uploads that would lower your total rolls or replace your save with an empty one. deliberate actions such as reset, import or "overwrite cloud with this device" are allowed, and the previous cloud save is kept as a restore point first
  • to stop syncing: log out (your local save stays on your device) or delete your account. there is no separate sync switch while you are logged in
restore points are extra copies of your cloud save that the server keeps so mistakes can be undone. they include automatic snapshots (taken at most every 15 minutes, the newest 24 kept), copies saved right before an overwrite (the newest 20 kept), best-progress copies (the 3 most recent kept), and copies of saves you discarded from a device (the newest 10 kept). you can see and restore them from your settings. the developer can also restore one from them to fix an error or when you ask.
restore points are a safety net, not a guarantee. they are limited in number and older ones are replaced automatically. cloud saves and restore points are provided as-is with no uptime guarantee, so we still recommend keeping local save exports as a backup.
when we moved to this system in october 2026, existing cloud saves were migrated and the better of your older copies was kept as your current cloud save, with the others kept as restore points. the previous backup files from the old system are no longer used and will be deleted.
leaderboard
the leaderboard is entirely opt-in and requires an account with at least 1,100 synced rolls. when you join:
  • your public name on the leaderboard is your account username
  • your stats are read from your synced cloud save and updated automatically whenever it syncs, so there is nothing to submit
  • the public leaderboard page refreshes about every 15 minutes, so numbers there can lag behind your game
  • these stats are publicly visible to anyone who visits the leaderboard page: total rolls, rarities collected, rarest rarity, playtime, points, achievements, and any tag the developer has added to your entry (for example for developers). they also appear on your public profile
  • your friends can also see you on their friends-only view of the leaderboard
  • you can opt out at any time. this permanently deletes your entry from our server (your cloud save is not affected)
your leaderboard identity is your account username. it is not anonymous once you opt in, since the whole point is a public, attributable leaderboard.
friends, messaging & public profiles
if you create an account, the following social features are available:
  • friends: you can send friend requests by username. the other player must accept before you're connected. either side can remove a friendship at any time
  • messaging: you can send direct and group messages to accounts by username, whether or not you're friends, and react to messages with emoji. messages are not end-to-end encrypted and are stored on our server so everyone in the conversation can read them later
  • public profile page: if you have an account, a public profile page exists at a url based on your username, showing whatever you've chosen to make visible: avatar, banner, bio, pronouns, social links, leaderboard stats and achievements (if you joined the leaderboard), and a rough online status such as "online now" or "active 3h ago". anyone with the link can view it, whether or not they're logged in
information you put in your bio, username, and public profile is visible to anyone who visits your profile page. don't include information you don't want to be public.
linking discord
you can optionally link a discord account to your auth's RNG account.
  • we store your discord user id, your discord username, and the time you linked it
  • our discord bot uses this link to check your total rolls and give you milestone roles in the auth's RNG discord server
  • you can unlink at any time. unlinking removes the stored discord details and stops future role updates. roles you already received are not removed automatically
  • discord has its own privacy policy that covers your discord account. we only receive your id and username when you link
reports & moderation
to keep the community safe, we keep some records:
  • reports: when a player reports someone, we store the reporter's username, the reported player's username, the reason, any context they add, and the time. only the developer sees reports
  • recent account activity: a short rolling log (the most recent 200 entries per account) of actions on your account, used only to review reports
  • moderation log: a record of actions the developer takes on accounts and the leaderboard (bans, renames, profile clearing, tag edits, stat overrides, save restores, and similar), with timestamps. the most recent 5000 entries are kept
these records contain usernames, so some can remain after an account is deleted. see data retention.
cookies & local storage
auth's RNG does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. game data is stored in your browser's localStorage (not cookies). localStorage data stays on your device and is only sent to our servers while you are logged in to an account.
you can clear your localStorage at any time through your browser settings. doing so will permanently delete your local save data. if you have an account, you can log in again and your cloud save will be restored, or pick an older copy from your restore points.
third-party services
the following third-party scripts or services are loaded or used when you visit the game. we do not control their data practices, so please review their own privacy policies:
  • cloudflare: serves the game frontend via cloudflare pages (global edge CDN) and proxies all backend api traffic via cloudflare tunnel. sees all request traffic (ip, headers) for both. cloudflare privacy policy
  • instant.page: preloading library served via cdnjs. may record requests to cdnjs infrastructure. instant.page
  • altcha: self-hosted, privacy-first captcha widget used on login and signup to prevent bot abuse. altcha does not use tracking, cookies, or third-party requests, and does not collect any personal data. the verification challenge is generated and checked entirely on our own servers. choosing altcha was intentional to protect the privacy of our users. altcha.org
  • librecounter: open-source hit counter. records a page view when you visit. librecounter.org
  • hitscounter.dev: visit counter. records a page view when you visit. hitscounter.dev
  • discord: only if you link a discord account or join our discord server. discord handles the link and our bot gives roles through discord's api. discord privacy policy
none of these services are used for advertising or behavioural tracking. they are open-source or privacy-oriented tools, but their own policies govern what they collect.
data retention
we retain data only as long as necessary:
  • accounts and profiles: kept until you delete your account
  • cloud saves: kept until you delete your account, or until you contact us requesting removal
  • restore points: replaced automatically as newer ones are made (see the limits under cloud saves) and deleted when you delete your account
  • leaderboard entries: kept until you opt out or delete your account, at which point they are permanently deleted from our server
  • login sessions: kept until you log out, revoke them, or delete your account, with a maximum of 20 per account (the oldest are removed first)
  • database backups: the developer takes daily backups of the account and cloud save databases and keeps the newest 14. data you delete can remain in these backups for up to 14 days after deletion, then it is gone
  • reports and moderation records: kept for as long as needed to handle reports and prevent abuse. they contain usernames, so they can remain after an account is deleted until they age out of the rolling logs
  • server logs: rotated and deleted on a rolling basis (typically within 30 days). not processed for profiling.
local browser data (localStorage) is entirely under your control and persists until you clear your browser data or use the in-game wipe feature!
